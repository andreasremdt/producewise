Mangos have deep roots in South and Southeast Asia. Centuries of cultivation and trade carried them far beyond their original home, bringing different varieties to tropical regions around the world.

That diversity is one reason a mango's skin color is such an unreliable ripeness guide. In the kitchen, a gentle squeeze is more useful than looking for a particular shade.
